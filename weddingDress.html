Custom Wedding Dress Package in Hillcrest

R300 - R400 per hour

Ebeth Dress Designs specialises in dresses for special occasions, and our wedding dress package is made for your biggest day. We start with a consultation to discuss your vision, venue and colour ideas, then create a custom wedding dress tailored to your measurements and style. Fittings and alterations are included to ensure the perfect fit.

What's Included

  • One-on-one design consultation
  • Sketches of your dress design for approval
  • Help choosing fabrics, lace and embellishments
  • Detailed measurements and a custom pattern
  • Multiple fittings and final alterations

Colour and Style

Your dress doesn't have to be white. We can create gowns in ivory, blush, red or any colour that suits you and your wedding theme, and we'll help you match the shade to your fabric and décor.

How It Works

  • Consultation: we talk through your vision, venue and budget.
  • Design: we agree on a style, silhouette, colour and fabrics.
  • Creation: your dress is made to your measurements.
  • Fittings: we adjust until it fits perfectly.

Good to Know

Most wedding dresses take several weeks to complete, so we recommend booking at least 4 to 6 months before your wedding date.
