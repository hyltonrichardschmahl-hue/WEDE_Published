Dress Alterations in Hillcrest

R200 per hour

Ebeth Dress Designs has been fitting and altering garments since 1995. Whether you have bought a dress, inherited one or need a favourite outfit adjusted, we alter it to fit you perfectly. Bring your garment in for a fitting and we will talk you through what can be done.

Alterations We Offer

  • Wedding, bridesmaid and formal dress alterations
  • Matric dance dress alterations
  • Hem adjustments and resizing
  • Adjustments to straps, sleeves and necklines
  • Veils and garters

How It Works

  • Fitting: we assess the garment and talk through what you would like changed.
  • Quote: we explain what is involved and how many hours it should take.
  • Alteration: the work is carried out with careful attention to the fabric and finish.
  • Final fitting: we check the fit and make any last adjustments.

Good to Know

Please book your alterations as early as you can, especially in busy periods such as wedding season and matric dance season, so there is time for fittings before your event.
