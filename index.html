Custom Wedding, Matric Dance & Formal Dresses in Hillcrest

Creating Something Special for Every Occasion

Welcome to Ebeth Dress Designs, a dressmaker based in Hillcrest, KwaZulu-Natal, where creativity, personal style, and careful craftsmanship come together to create beautiful garments for life's important moments. We specialise in designing and making clothing for a variety of occasions, including weddings, Matric Dances, formal events, and other special celebrations.

Whether you already have a specific design in mind or need guidance in developing your ideas, we work with you to create a garment that suits your vision. Our services range from custom-made dresses and formal outfits to alterations and accessories, including veils and garters. We can also assist clients with sourcing and selecting suitable materials when needed.

We understand that every client is different, which is why we take the time to understand what you are looking for before creating your garment. Our aim is to make the process enjoyable and stress-free while ensuring that the final result is something you will be proud to wear. Explore our services and discover how Ebeth Dress Designs can help bring your ideal outfit to life.
