About Ebeth Dress Designs: Experience, Passion and Personal Service

Based in Hillcrest, KwaZulu-Natal, Ebeth Dress Designs is built on years of experience and a passion for dressmaking. Ebeth has been working in the Bridal, Formal, and Matric Dance Wear industry since 1995, developing a wealth of knowledge about garment design, fabrics, fitting, and construction. This experience has helped shape the way the business approaches each project and has provided a strong understanding of what is required to create quality garments.

Since 2021, Ebeth has worked alongside another designer, adding further knowledge and experience to the business. Between the designers, there is over 50 years of combined designing experience, providing a strong foundation for the work carried out by the business.

The business has a strong emphasis on consistency, communication, and making clients feel valued. Rather than treating customers as simply another order, the focus is on building a relationship with each client and understanding their needs throughout the process. This allows the designers to make informed decisions about the garment while ensuring that the client's ideas remain central to the finished product.

Over the years, this approach has allowed Ebeth Dress Designs to provide a wide range of services, including dress design and pattern making, wedding and bridesmaid dresses, Matric Dance dresses, formal wear, M.O.B. and M.O.G. outfits, alterations, veils, and garters. The business continues to place quality workmanship and customer satisfaction at the centre of its work.
